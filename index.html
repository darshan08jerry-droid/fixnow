FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and how we work.

How quickly can a technician arrive at my home?

We guarantee a technician at your door within 24 hoursin Coimbatore,Chennai,Madurai,Tirupur,Erode. For same-day bookings made before 5 PM, we ensure evening delivery. Emergency calls are prioritised at all times.

Is the free inspection really free? No hidden charges?

Yes — 100% free. Our technician visits, inspects your appliance, diagnoses the issue, and gives you a fixed quote. You only pay if you approve the repair. Zero commitment, zero pressure.

Which appliance brands do you service?

We service all major brands — Samsung, LG, Mitsubishi, Whirlpool, O Genral Godrej, Daikin, Voltas, Blue Star, Hitachi, Panasonic, Haier, IFB, Bosch, Kent, Aquaguard, Pureit, and many more. If it's a home appliance, we fix it.

What does the 90-day warranty cover?

The 100-day warranty covers the exact issue we repaired and the parts we replaced. If the same problem recurs within 90 days, we come back and fix it completely free — no questions asked.

Do you use original spare parts?

Absolutely. We use only OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer) certified spare parts for all repairs. We never use duplicate or low-quality parts that could damage your appliance or void your manufacturer warranty.

Which cities we have cover ?

We cover all major cities Coimabtore, Chennai, Madurai, Tirupur, Erode, and surrounding areas.

What are your service hours?

Our regular service hours are 9 AM to 9 PM, 7 days a week including Sundays and public holidays. For genuine emergencies, our WhatsApp support line is active 24×7 so you can reach us anytime.

How can I pay? Do you accept UPI?

We accept all payment methods — cash, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and debit/credit cards. Payment is collected after the repair is done and you're completely satisfied with the work.
