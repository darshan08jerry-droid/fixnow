Account and Data Deletion

FixNow users may request deletion of their account and associated personal information. Here's how, and what happens next.

How to request deletion What happens next Information that may be retained

How to request deletion

Send an email to fixnowcoimbatore@gmail.com from the email address registered with your FixNow account.

Use the subject:

FixNow Account Deletion Request

Include:

  • Full name
  • Registered email address
  • Registered mobile number
  • Account role: customer, technician, administrator, or other
  • A clear request to delete the account

You may also contact +91 80986 06019 for assistance.

Email a deletion request WhatsApp us

What happens next

1

Identity verification

FixNow may contact you to verify your identity before processing the request.

2

Account disabled or deleted

Your account will be disabled or deleted after verification.

3

Data removed where possible

Information that is no longer required will be deleted or anonymized.

4

Processing time

Requests are normally processed within 30 days.

You'll receive a confirmation once your request has been processed. If you have any questions in the meantime, reach out to fixnowcoimbatore@gmail.com.

Information that may be retained

FixNow may retain limited records where required for:

  • Bills, tax, accounting, and payment records
  • Completed service history and warranties
  • Technician attendance, payroll, and employment records
  • Fraud prevention and security investigations
  • Pending complaints, disputes, or legal claims
  • Compliance with applicable law

Retained information will remain protected and will not be used for unrelated purposes.

See our full Privacy Policy for more detail on how we handle your data.
